# Credits

## Movement
The movement system is ported from **faith-runner** by tnrjns
(https://github.com/tnrjns/faith-runner), used and adapted with the author's permission
(granted by email, 2026-10-08; a copy of the email is kept privately).
faith-runner's values and rules are in turn derived from **Mirror's Edge** (DICE / EA).
No Mirror's Edge files are included in this project.

## Courses
The greybox and Rooftops courses (Unreal/Runline/Data/Levels) are faith-runner's level designs
(`greybox.rs`, `rooftops.rs`), exported as box lists by tools/golden.

## Template content
Unreal Engine's First Person template (Epic Games): the project's starting point; its
`LevelPrototyping` grid material colours the courses.
